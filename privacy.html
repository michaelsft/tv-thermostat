TV Thermostat privacy policy

TV Thermostat is a personal app used only by its owner, to see and control their own Google Nest thermostat on their own TV.

What it uses

With the owner's permission, given through Google's sign-in, the app reads the thermostat's temperature, humidity and settings, and changes its temperature and mode when asked to. It uses Google's Smart Device Management API for this and nothing else.

Where it's kept

The app's Google sign-in and the latest thermostat reading are stored only on the owner's TV. Nothing is sent anywhere except to Google, to read and control the thermostat.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to or seen by anyone else. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the TV app (hold OK on the dial and choose Disconnect) or from the Google account's connected apps and Google Home's partner connections.
